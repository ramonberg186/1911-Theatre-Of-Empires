Introduction

An Alternate History

Following Japan's annexation of Korea, tensions began to rise in the Pacific. With the British Empire openly supporting Japan's expansion, the United States grew increasingly concerned about Japanese expanding influence in the Pacific and started preparing for war. Facing the growing influence of Britain and Japan, Germany and the United States formed an alliance to challenge them!

Germany invaded Denmark to secure control of the Baltic Sea. At Germany's request, the Netherlands launched an invasion of Antwerp out of fear that a British naval blockade could threaten the Central Powers' access to the seas.

Across the Atlantic, in response to growing tensions, Brazil strengthened its diplomatic ties with Portugal, who aligned themselves with the Entente.

Before the United States could complete its military preparations, Japan declared war and launched an all out attack against American possessions in the Pacific. Japan also invaded China, opening a front in East Asia.

In response, Germany declared war on France and Russia. The Austrian Empire and Ottoman Empire pledged their full support to Germany while also launching a full scale attack on Italy.

The world is in a full blown conflict. The fate of the Empires will be decided on the battlefield!

Theatre of Empires takes place all over the world! The map includes twelve playable Empires which compete across the globe! Every campaign feels different. With more than 10 unique units and researchable technologies, the replayability is infinite! Defend your positions with Landmines and Trenches, conquer your enemies with Flamethrowers and Tanks, or rule the skies with Zeppelins, Observation Balloons, and the powerful Nighthawk Fighters!

About This Rulebook

This rulebook is divided into 16 chapters to help you understand the game. The opening chapters explain most of the basics of the map. Chapters 5 to 10 explain units and combat mechanics. Chapters 11 to 13 explain canals, technology, and national objectives. Chapter 14 provides strategy tips for each playable nation followed by game settings and special rules in chapter 15 and credits in chapter 16.

2. Victory Conditions

Victory Requirements

Victory is achieved by controlling your own and your opponents capitals! Crush your enemies!

Victory Requirements Central Powers

The Central powers win the war if they collectively control at least 11 of the 14 capitals.

Victory Requirements Entente

The Entente powers win the war if they collectively control at least 10 of the 14 capitals.

Note: US and the Dutch each have two capitals (Washington DC and San Francisco for US, Amsterdam and Batavia for the Dutch). When one capital is conquered, the conqueror plunders all on hand PU's, but the nation that lost a capital will still be able to purchase and place units so long as they control at least one capital.

Nations take their turns in a fixed order.

  2. Purchase Units

  4. Combat

4. Economy & Production

Purchasing Units

Players can spend their PUs to purchase land, naval or air units. Each unit has a different production cost, unit costs can be found in chapters 5,6 and 7.

Factories & (Sea)Mines

Factories can produce new units. Production capacity is limited by the PU value of the territory.

Trenches, Landmines, and Seamines do not need a factory to be placed. Up to three Trenches per turn can be placed on any controlled territory with no total limit. Landmines can be placed on controlled territories at a rate of one per turn and a limit of 10 per territory. Seamines can only be placed in Sea Zones and you are required to have a Combat ship from your nation in the sea zone to build one per turn with a limit of 10 in the territory.

Factories can be damaged by strategic bombing raids. For more information, see chapter 9.

Objectives & Blockades

National Objectives provide additional PUs when their requirements are fulfilled. For more information see chapter 13!

Naval Blockades reduce your income by disrupting your ports. for more information see chapter 10!

8. Combat Mechanics

Support Fire

Support Fire increases the Attack value of a friendly unit by 1.

First Strike

Units with First Strike perform one additional attack before regular combat begins using their normal Attack value. Hits scored are applied before the enemy units can return fire. After their First Strike attack, these units participate in regular combat as normal.

Siege Attack

Siege Attack allows units to damage enemy Trenches before regular combat begins, using their normal Attack value. Any hits are applied before the battle starts. After resolving their Siege Attack these units will participate in regular combat as normal.

Debuff

Debuff reduces the Attack value of affected enemy units by 1.

9. Air Mechanics

Strategic Bombing

Units with the Bombing ability can conduct strategic bombing raids against enemy factories. The bombing roll is equal to the amount of economic damage inflicted on the enemy.

Factories can receive Bombing damage up to the PU value of the territory. Any damage is automatically removed from the opposing player's total PU count, repairing the factories instantly.

Air Interception

Units with the Intercept ability can intercept enemy bombers during strategic Bombing raids. Interceptors use their Air Defense value to attack enemy bombers once.

If the interceptor gets a hit, the enemy bomber is destroyed. Otherwise, the interceptor retreats, and the Bombing raid continues.

Friendly interceptors can also escort bombers, using their Air Attack value to defend them against enemy interceptors.

Anti-Aircraft Defense

AA Guns can attack all enemy aircraft during combat and strategic Bombing raids. They have a First Strike attack value of 2.

AA Guns can also apply a Debuff to up to 10 enemy aircraft, reducing their Attack value by 1.

10. Naval Warfare & Blockades

Submerge

Submarines can submerge before or during combat removing themselves from the battle. Submerged submarines cannot be attacked by aircraft and do not block enemy ships.

Submarines have First Strike attacks when their Submerge ability is active.

Detect

Destroyers have the Detect ability. Detech ability disables the Submerge ability from enemy submarines.

Naval Bombardment

Ships with the Bombard ability can attack enemy land units before regular land combat begins. Their bombardment value is equal to their Attack value and has the ability to First Strike. Opponents can choose which units take the hit in Bombardments. Naval bombardment is limited to the number of ground units participating in the amphibious assault. For example, if three ground units participate in an amphibious assault, a maximum of three ships may bombard the defending territory.

Amphibious Assaults

Units with the Amphibious ability get +1 attack while conducting an Amphibious assault.

Transport Capacity

Transports can carry land units with a combined Transport Cost of up to 5. Each land unit has its own Transport Cost, as listed in Chapter 5. For example, a Transport can carry one Infantry (2) and one Artillery (3) at the same time. Transports can be taken as casualties. Battlecruisers can transport one Infantry.

Naval Blockades

Blockades allow enemy ships to reduce a nation's income. Some specific sea zones are designated as blockade zones, allowing enemy ships to blockade adjacent land territories.

Each warship rolls one die, except submarines, which roll two dice. A roll of 1, 2, or 3 inflicts PU damage equal to the number rolled. Rolls of 4, 5, or 6 inflict no damage.

The total damage cannot exceed the income generated by the affected territories.

11. Canals

Canals and straits connect sea zones. Controlling the territories allows you or your allies to pass through them. Newly captured canals become usable to the capturing nation on its next turn, while allies may use them on their next turn and even within the same round.

  • Bosphorus & Dardanelles (Sea Zone 67: Black Sea / Sea Zone 68: Aegean Sea)
  • Suez Canal (Cairo-Suez / Sea Zone 70: Eastern Mediterranean / Sea Zone 75: Red Sea)
  • Straits of Denmark (Denmark, Sweden / Sea Zone 13: Skagerrak / Sea Zone 12: Baltic Sea)

12. Technology

Researching new technologies allows you to unlock new units and expand your strategic capabilities. There are 12 different technologies, divided into 4 categories:

  1. Offensive Tactics (red)
  2. Defensive Tactics (pink)
  3. Naval Units (blue)
  4. Air Units (purple)

Each category has 3 unique technologies that will help you in your goal to achieve total domination!

Researching Technology

At the beginning of your turn you can select which technology you want to research. Each Research Token costs 4 PU's. The game engine rolls a dice for you. If the result is equal or lower than the number of Research Tokens you have you unluck the selected technology at the beginning of your next turn!

The engine uses low luck, every 6 research tokens guerantees one succesfull technology breakthrough. You can only unlock one technology per turn. Your research tokens are saved and carried over to your next turn if your research fails.

All Technologies

Offensive Tactics

Gas

Researching Gas unlocks the gas unit. Gas is a very strong suicide unit. Gas excels in killing large stacks of units which are protected by a stack of Trenches. Countries who should consider investing in gas are: Germany, Austria, and Japan. Tip: If you're going for a “Kill France First” strategy as the Central Powers, a minor nation, such as the Dutch, can research gas and weaken enemy positions for the Germans to deliver the final blow!

Armour

Researching Armour unlocks the production of Tanks. Tanks are very strong units with the ability Siege Attack. Tanks are really strong if you want to quickly blitz through enemy territory. Tanks are very effective on the offense but lack real defensive abilities; make sure you defend them well! Countries who should consider investing in Tanks are: Germany, Ottomans, and Austria!

Flamethrowers

Researching Flamethrowers unlocks the production of Flamethrower units. Flamethrowers are strong units with the Siege Attack ability. Flamethrowers are an excellent pick if your enemy bought a lot of trenches to defend their infantry. Countries who should consider investing in Flamethrowers: Germany, Austria, Ottomans, Britain, Japan, and USA! Tip: Flamethrowers and Artillery is a deadly combo!

Defensive Tactics

Heavy Guns

Researching Heavy Guns unlocks the production of Heavy Guns. Heavy Guns are the backbone of your defensive positions! They have a high defensive value but are fragile if left alone. Countries who should consider investing in Heavy Guns: France, Russia and China! Tip: Use Heavy Guns together with Trenches, Landmines, and Conscripts to build the ultimate defensive line!

Conscripts

Researching Conscripts unlocks the production of Conscript units. Conscripts are very cheap cannon fodder units. They have 0 offensive value but are valuable additions to your defense, especially for countries with small economies. Russia, China and Italy already start the game with conscripts unlocked! Tip: Use Conscripts together with Heavy Guns, Trenches and Landmines to build the ultimate defensive line!

Propaganda

Propaganda gives you 3 free Conscripts at the end of each turn on your capital. This tech is great if you need a steady supply of Conscripts. Tip: Use this tech for countries such as China and Italy.

Sea Units

Flying Boats

Researching Flying Boats unlocks the production of Flying Boats. Flying Boats are flexible Air units who can land on sea but not on land! Flying Boats cannot be blocked by enemy ships and have high mobility! Flying Boats are able to move after combat, but they are not required to. Countries who should consider investing in this tech: USA, Japan, and Britain. Tip: Flying Boats are great at sniping smaller fleets or catching your opponents off guard but very weak in big naval stacks!

Carriers

Researching Carriers unlocks the production of Aircraft Carriers. Aircraft Carriers are the backbone of any mobile naval force; this unit is great in a big naval stack! Countries who should consider unlocking carriers are: Japan, USA, Britain, and Portugal. Tip: Carriers can carry 2 normal Fighters or 1 Nighthawk Fighter!

Marines

Researching Marines unlocks the production of Marine units. Marines specialize in Amphibious combat, they receive a +1 attack bonus when conducting an amphibious assault. Countries who should consider unlocking Marines are: USA, Japan, and Britain! Tip: Use Marines together with Artillery!

Air units

Zeppelin

Researching Zeppelin unlocks the production of Zeppelins. Zeppelins are huge air units with a very long range and the ability to conduct Bombing Raids against enemy factories. Germany starts the game with Zeppelins unlocked. Countries who should consider unlocking Zeppelins are: Britain, USA, Japan, and Austria. Tip: If your opponent doesn't have many Anti Air Guns, it might be worth investing in Zeppelins!

Nighthawk

Researching Nighthawk unlocks the production of the Nighthawk Fighter aircraft. The Nighthawk is a huge upgrade compared to a normal fighter but comes at a greater cost! Nighthawk is the strongest Air unit but lacks real mobility and is very expensive. Combine Nighthawks together with infantry or conscripts to achieve maximum value! Countries who should consider unlocking Nighthawks: Germany, USA, Britain, and Japan! Tip: Nighthawks can intercept enemy Zeppelins before they bomb your factories!

Observation Balloon

Researching Observation Balloon unlocks the production of the Observation Balloon unit. The Observation Balloon is a support unit. It can provide Support Fire for up to 3 friendly units but has no Attack value on its own. Countries who should consider unlocking the Observation Balloon: Germany, Britain, Austria, Ottomans, Japan, Russia, and France! Tip: use your balloons together with cheap Conscripts to get an economical counter offensive force!

14. Nation Profiles & Strategy Tips

Each Empire enters the conflict with its own strengths and weaknesses. This chapter is a small introduction for each Empire, explaining its strategic goals and giving some basic tips.

Japan

Background: Japan is a growing Empire with big ambitions across the Pacific and Asia.

Strategic Goals: Expand your influence across the Pacific, China, and into the Dutch East Indies. Secure valuable islands and threaten the United States!

Strategy Tips: You're up first! Try to secure some easy kills against the unsuspecting Americans. There are multiple different roads to victory. You can try to secure China or push deep into the Dutch East Indies! Remember to keep the United States at bay!

China

Background: China faces a period of political instability while foreign powers compete for influence in China. China is keen to retake their important ports, but be careful: the Russians and Japanese are next door.

Strategic Goals: Defend your homeland. China is in a very tough spot. Try to secure your factories.

Strategy Tips: Focus on defending, but don't be too passive. If Russia and Japan give you some breathing room, take advantage of it and try to push into Russia or India!

United States

Background: The United States is a global power with growing influence in the Pacific and North America. If you play your cards correctly, you can turn this nation into one of the biggest Empires.

Strategic Goals: United States start the game weak but can get strong very quickly. Try to achieve your objectives and kick the British out of Canada.

Strategy Tips: Take British Columbia turn 1 and try to take the Canadian factories as soon as possible. Make sure you defend your coastal shores against the Japanese, Portuguese and Brits. After taking down Canada, you can push into Mexico for neutral PUs and try to knock out either Japan or Britain.

Britain

Background: Britain is a large overseas Empire. Britain is the most difficult nation to play and requires you to assist your allies on all fronts.

Strategic Goals: Protect your key territorial holdings such as Cairo-Suez, Cape Town, and Sydney. Wherever possible, assist Japan in the Pacific, put pressure on the Dutch and Germans (both in Europe and their colonies) and try to stop the Americans from expanding.

Strategy Tips: Your empire is widespread, so choose your priorities carefully. Use your big navy to secure naval dominance in key regions, try to secure Africa, and help kill the Dutch East Indies with the Japanese. Trying to hold Canada from falling to the USA might also be worth your time. Land units into Europe to assist the French.

Germany

Background: Germany is a major Empire with big colonial ambitions overseas.

Strategic Goals: Secure your colonies, prevent your ports from being blockaded, and focus your efforts on either France or Russia.

Strategy Tips: You cannot kill France and Russia, you have to make a choice who you will conquer first. Russia is a vast country with a huge production base, while France is a close neighbour with a small starting army. Try to secure your colonies or maybe even push into Scandinavia. Good teamplay with your allied nations is crucial for success.

France

Background: France is an established colonial Empire struggling to keep their influence in Africa/Asia.

Strategic Goals: Defend Paris, help Italy, and try to kick Germany out of Africa.

Strategy Tips: Protecting Paris is very important, make sure Germany can't walk through France unimpeded. Help Italy whenever possible and try to retake your lost colonies in Africa.

Austria

Background: Austria is a multinational empire whose goals are mainly to push into Russia and to conquer Rome.

Strategic Goals: Work together with Germany, secure the Balkans, and push into Italy and Russia.

Strategy Tips: Take Venice turn 1 and Belgrade turn 2 if possible. Work together with Germany in the East and push to Rome and Kiev.

Italy

Background: Italy is a young European Empire looking to strengthen their colonial ambitions beyond their own borders.

Strategic Goals: Protect your territory, secure the Med, and assist your allies.

Strategy Tips: Protect your territory and try to secure naval dominance in the Med together with Britain and France. If possible, try to assist in Africa.

Ottoman Empire

Background: The Ottoman Empire is a very important Empire due to its strategic location between Europe and the Middle East.

Strategic Goals: Secure the Balkans and try to secure vital territories such as Cairo, Caucasus and Tehran.

Strategy Tips: Your territory covers several possible fronts. Make sure you prevent the British from pushing into the Middle East. Put pressure on the Russian flank and try to push into neutral Iran to secure your bonus.

Russia

Background: Russia is a huge empire stretching from Eastern Europe to the Pacific, with enemies on all fronts and long distances between its strategic locations.

Strategic Goals: Defend your western flank, prevent your key cities from falling, and defend your fragile flanks. Help Japan contain the Chinese army.

Strategy Tips: Use your vast territory to your advantage and buy time for your allies to assist you. At the same time, don't be too passive.

Netherlands

Background: The Netherlands is a smaller Empire with strategic locations all over the globe.

Strategic Goals: Protect the homeland, preserve colonial territories and try to assist Germany wherever possible.

Strategy Tips: Make sure you don't lose Batavia or Amsterdam. You can be a can opener for Germany into France or you can help slow down Japan in the Pacific.

Portugal

Background: Portugal is a small Empire with a big presence in South America.

Strategic Goals: Assist your allies in Africa and defend yourself against America.

Strategy Tips: You have a factory in Rio which can be used to transport troops into Africa. Build ships in Lisbon to help Britain against America.

15. Game Settings & Special Rules

Our map uses specific settings that influence combat, production, and certain units.

Anti-Aircraft Fire

Players cannot select which aircraft are destroyed by anti-aircraft fire. Casualties are selected automatically and randomly by the game engine.

Aircraft Carriers & Fighters

Newly purchased fighters can be instantly placed on existing aircraft carriers. Existing fighters positioned on land next to an aircraft carrier produced this round can be moved over in the same round if desired.

Naval Combat & Submarines

Submarines may retreat before a naval battle begins when there is no enemy destroyer present such as described in Chapter 10.

Submarines do not prevent enemy naval movement through a sea zone.

Battleship Damage & Repairs

Battleships and Trenches take two hits before they are destroyed. Unit damage is repaired at the start of the owning player's turn.

Amphibious Assault

Partial amphibious retreats are allowed. Ground units that entered the battle from adjacent territories, rather than from transports, may retreat. Naval Bombardment is limited to the number of ground units participating in the assault. For more information on naval Bombardment and amphibious assaults, see Chapter 10.

Technology Development

Technology research is enabled by default. Research follows the technology rules described in Chapter 12.

If you want to play without technology, you can disable it in the game options.

Capitals

Capitals can be looted, resulting in a loss of all the banked PU's. Once a capital falls a nation cannot produce any new units. The Dutch and USA have 2 capitals, both can be looted. If one capital falls they both can continue the fight until their final capital falls!

Optional Game Settings

Most game settings are fixed and cannot be changed. A small number of options, including Low Luck, Technology Development, and bidding, can be turned off.
