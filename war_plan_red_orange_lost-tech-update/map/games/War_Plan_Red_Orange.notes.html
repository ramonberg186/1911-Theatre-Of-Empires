Tech Unit Overview:


Heavy Guns


Land unit, 5 cost, 2/4/1. Artillery. 5 transporting cost.

Tanks


Land unit, 6 cost, 4/2/2. 3 transporting cost.

Conscripts


Land unit, 17 cost for 8, 0/1/1. 2 transporting cost.

Gas


Land unit, 4 cost, 4/0/3. Hits once on attack, then suicides. Destroyed when captured.

Zeppelins


Air unit, 14 cost, 2/1/6. Can perform bombing raids. Cannot land on carriers.

Late Fighters


Air unit, 10 cost, 3/4/4. 1 carrier cost.

Aircraft Carriers


Sea unit, 12 cost, 1/1/2. 2 carrying capacity.

Mines


Mines are a little interesting. There are landmines and seamines.
Landmines can be placed in any territory you started the turn controlling, up to 3 at a time. They cost 2 and will fire at 4 once, then suicide. Landmines and trenches share a pool and you can place up to 3 of any combination of both in a territory.
Seamines can be placed in any convoy zone you started the turn controlling or any sea zone adjacent to a land territory you started the turn controlling. They cost 4 and will fire at 4, then suicide.
In both cases, mines only fire if there are other units with defensive capabilities in the territory. If not, they will be killed immediately upon the start of combat. However, they will block movement for any units with mobility.
