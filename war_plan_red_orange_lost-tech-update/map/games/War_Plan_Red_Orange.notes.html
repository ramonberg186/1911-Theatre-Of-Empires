Tech Overview:


Economic Reforms


Gives 9 pus free units on capitol per turn (default is inf).

Working Women


Knocks down the cost of artillery to 3.5 and fighters to 7.

Working Women


Knocks down the cost of artillery to 3.5 and fighters to 7.

War Bonds


2d6 extra pus per turn.

Industry


Increases produciton capacity of territories with at least 2 production by 2.

Science


Gives 1 free tech token per turn.

Fleet Action


Gives Battleships and battlecruisers 1 attack bonus.

Convoys


Gives destroyers and cruisers 1 defense bonus.

Sub Warfare


Gives submarines 1 attack bonus.

Dockyards


Reduces cost of all naval units by 1.

Offensive Tactics


Gives cavalry 1 movement bonus.

Creeping Barrage


1 attack bonus to artillery.

Heavy Guns


Land unit, 5 cost, 2/4/1. Artillery. 5 transporting cost.

Armour


Land unit, 5 cost, 4/2/2. 3 transporting cost. Bunker buster: rolls 2d6 at 3 to instantly destroy trenches at the beginning of battle.

Defensive Tactics


Unlocks conscripts, Land unit, 2 cost, 0/1/1. 2 transporting cost.

Gas


Land unit, 4 cost, 5/0/3. Hits once on attack, then suicides. Destroyed when captured.

Zeppelins


Air unit, 12 cost, 2/1/6. Can perform bombing raids. Cannot land on carriers.

Late Fighters


Air unit, 10 cost, 3/4/4. 1 carrier cost. Supports infantry

Aircraft Carriers


Sea unit, 12 cost, 1/1/2. 2 carrying capacity.

Mines


Mines are a little interesting. There are landmines and seamines.
Landmines can be placed in any territory you started the turn controlling, up to 3 at a time. They cost 2 and will fire at 4 once, then suicide. Landmines and trenches share a pool and you can place up to 3 of any combination of both in a territory.
Seamines can be placed in any convoy zone you started the turn controlling or any sea zone adjacent to a land territory you started the turn controlling. They cost 4 and will fire at 4, then suicide.
In both cases, mines only fire if there are other units with defensive capabilities in the territory. If not, they will be killed immediately upon the start of combat. However, they will block movement for any units with mobility.
